Hi, I’m Wendy. I design apps and websites that people just get, no instructions needed.

How it started

During the COVID-19 lockdown 😷, school was on pause and I wanted to learn a skill. I decided on tech, but I didn’t want to write a single line of code 🙅🏾‍♀️. Then I found UI/UX and taught myself the basics. A couple of years later, after finishing school, I got serious: bootcamps, tech events, connecting with other designers, and eventually a full-time job as a designer.

The plot twist

At the end of 2025 I started learning a new skill alongside design: tech support. By 2026 it was my full-time role, and I fell completely in love with it 😍. Support turned out to be a front-row seat to how people actually use products. Every confused message is a design brief in disguise, and suddenly design and support weren’t two different jobs anymore.

Right now

Now I’m back to designing full time, with that support experience in every decision. And I’m open to my next full-time role. 👀

The one rule I design by

“Where would I look if I was in a hurry?”

The same questions kept coming up, and most of them weren’t the client’s fault. The answer was usually right there on the screen, just not where anyone would think to look. So now, before I call any design finished, I ask myself that one question.

Where it shows up in my work

OrvoThe fee, the network and the total sit on one review screen, before anyone is asked for a PIN. ZukkyWhat you’ll pay sits right under where you’re going, so the price is never a surprise. SeatsMoney is held until you confirm you’re in, so nobody has to ask where their payment went.
