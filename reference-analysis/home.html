    My design process

    1. 01 / 04

      Spark

      Every project starts with the real problem, the spark worth solving. I find it through people, data and a close look at what exists.

      • User interviews
      • Product audit
      • Competitor teardown
    2. 02 / 04

      Sketch

      I sketch fast and loose: flows, rough screens and many options, so the best idea wins before any pixel is polished.

      • User flows
      • Low-fi wireframes
      • Ideation
    3. 03 / 04

      Shape

      The chosen idea becomes a real interface and a working prototype, tested with people and refined until it feels right.

      • UI design
      • Prototypes
      • Usability testing
    4. 04 / 04

      Ship

      I hand over a tidy design system and stay through the build, so what launches is what was designed.

      • Design system
      • Developer handoff
      • Launch QA
